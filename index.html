Adnan Armouti

CS PhD Candidate, Cornell University
Graduate Committee: Prof. Rajalakshmi Nandakumar (chair), Andrew Owens, Steve Marschner, Alyosha Molnar

I develop physics-based generative systems for millimeter-wave radars to enable radio frequency perception and unlock capabilities beyond ordinary cameras. I work on differentiable rendering and foundation models for inverse problems in FMCW radar imaging, with applications in 3D vision, human-centric sensing and autonomous robotics. My work appears at ECCV, SIGGRAPH and MobiCOM.
